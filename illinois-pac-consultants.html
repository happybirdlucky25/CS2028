How this list was built

  • Illinois means the PAC reported the payee's address with recipient_state = "IL". It's a mailing address, not proof the work happened in Illinois. The same firm can appear under another state elsewhere: the Murphy Mobilization Fund paid Aisle 518 Strategies $1,089,763 at a Washington, DC address, and Mark Kelly for Senate paid it $12.95M at an Arlington, VA address. Neither is counted here.
  • Consultant means a payee with at least one payment described as consulting (strategic, fundraising, digital or compliance). All of that payee's payments are included. Aisle 518 Strategies is included as a political consulting firm, although most of its payments here are media buys.
  • Tagged rows are payments to a consultant that weren't described as consulting, such as media buys, direct mail production and postage, web hosting or a travel reimbursement.
  • Left out: design and production vendors (Change Media Group, Madval Design, Apollo Artistry, Fox Frame Productions, about $45K), travel, card fees, refunds to donors and contributions to candidates or parties.
  • Not found in Illinois: Ready for the Fight (Pritzker), In This Together (Beshear), Juntos (Gallego), American Mobilization (Murphy), Courage to Change (Ocasio-Cortez), the Murphy Mobilization Fund, the Mark Kelly Victory Fund and Purpose PAC (Booker) paid no Illinois consultants in this data. Independent expenditures (Schedule E) aren't included.
  • PAC links to people follow news reporting and the donor and address patterns in the filings. Win the Era, Fight Like Hell and Unity First are not formally sponsored leadership PACs, and the Senate campaigns are included because their candidates are possible 2028 contenders, so treat those links as "associated with".
  • Addresses come from the PACs' raw electronic filings (.fec files on docquery.fec.gov), because the FEC's data export has only city and ZIP. Each consultant had the same address on every filing. Street addresses for the individuals and for firms that filed at a home or apartment address are left off this page but are in illinois_consultants_entities.csv.
  • Websites were found by web search. Only Authentic's (authentic.org) is confirmed. Aisle 518 Strategies is described elsewhere as a Washington, DC firm with a remote staff, so its Chicago address may be a satellite office.
  • Only itemized payments appear. Filings run through 30 Sep 2026.
Data: Federal Election Commission, Schedule B itemized disbursements. Compiled 24 Nov 2026.
