Strategy Consultants: 2028 Democratic Field

Research as of Nov 24, 2026. Covers the 42 strategy and general consultants paid at least $5,000 by committees tied to possible 2028 Democratic presidential candidates, from FEC filings through 30 Sep 2026.

Overview

About 10 of the 42 are independent consulting firms. Roughly 20 are the candidates' own senior aides and political staff, paid on contract rather than salary. About 12 could not be identified from public sources.

  • Independent firms: Outperform Strategies, Hilltop Public Solutions and Arc Initiatives (Beshear); Mercury Public Affairs, its campaign arm Renaissance Campaign Strategies, and Silver Strategies (Booker); Steamship, Jimmy Dahman Strategies and Kate Conway LLC (Buttigieg).
  • Staff paid as consultants: most of the Buttigieg individuals are Win the Era PAC staff who worked on Pete for America 2020 and at his Department of Transportation. Newsom's Lindsey Cobia, Kelly's Jen Cox and Gallego's Raphael Chavez-Fernandez are each a senior aide to the candidate.
  • Continuity: these operations rely on long-standing teams. Win the Era has at least nine Pete 2020 alumni on payroll, and Beshear has worked with Eric Hyers since 2019.

Beshear: In This Together PAC

Eric Hyers oversees the super PAC and Beshear's political team (Kentucky Lantern). The PAC also pays national firms.

  • Outperform Strategies ($116K): Hyers managed Beshear 2019 and 2023 and was AAPC Campaign Manager of the Year for 2023. He also ran Raimondo 2014, Bullock 2016, Biden Michigan 2020 and Harris North Carolina 2024. The team includes Nicole Kayner (communications and debate prep) and Colin Lauderdale (campaign manager). The firm runs Save America Fund, an independent-expenditure super PAC that spent $8M+ for Tim Ryan in 2022 (firm site).
  • Hilltop Public Solutions ($56K): founded 2006. Its offices are led by Peter Kauffmann (NY), Courtni Pugh (Southern California), Craig Hughes (CO), Barrett Kaiser (MT), Jane Slusser and Brendan McPhillips (PA) and Dayna Lurie Parramore (WA). Past work includes the ACA coalition (~$100M) and the NFLPA lockout (firm site).
  • Arc Initiatives ($30K): founded 2013, partnered with True Blue Strategies, runs Arc North Star in Minnesota (PR Newswire).
  • Bellwether Strategy Group ($30K, Louisville): not identified.

Booker: Cory Booker for Senate and Purpose PAC

  • Mercury Public Affairs ($80K) and Renaissance Campaign Strategies ($27.5K): Renaissance is Mercury's campaign arm (City & State). Mercury's CEO is John Gallagher and its president is Michael Soliman. Partners include Cheri Bustos and Toby Moffett.
  • Silver Strategies ($45K): Adam Silverstein, Wyckoff, NJ (Bergen CC).
  • Libra Labs ($42K): a small software and technology firm in Northern Virginia, also paid by Virginia and Michigan Democrats (VPAP).
  • Michael Frosolone ($36K): Booker 2020 Iowa state director. Earlier he ran Iowa House Democrats' political operation (Ballotpedia).
  • Diana Bowen Creative ($27K): a video editor whose work includes Booker's Instagram reels, the DNC, Hillary for America and Demand Justice (portfolio).
  • Not identified: MYC Strategies ($15K, Wenatchee, WA) and Ayinde Martin ($12K, Newark).

Newsom: Campaign for Democracy

  • Lindsey Cobia ($81K): Newsom's long-time deputy chief of staff and senior political adviser (Capitol Weekly). She is also treasurer of the super PAC that paid her.
  • View Partners ($90K, one payment in Sep 2026): a California LLC (OpenCorporates). Its work is not described publicly.

Murphy: American Mobilization PAC

  • Cormorant Strategies Group ($81K): no public website content or principals.
  • Sarah Baron ($36K, fundraising consulting): possibly the DC campaign strategist who was Unrig Our Economy's campaign director and ran campaigns at Climate Power and Warren 2020 (InfluenceWatch). This match is not confirmed.

Moore, Whitmer, Kelly, Gallego

  • Carbide Strategies ($62K, Oakland, MD, Moore): not identified.
  • Whitmer's Fight Like Hell PAC: LMJ LLC ($38K), Baker-Hall & Associates ($22K) and Cee Maul ($5K) were not identified. Reese Drilling ($25K) was Whitmer's chief speechwriter.
  • Jen Cox ($25K, Kelly): Kelly's chief of staff since 2020 and a Harris 2024 Arizona senior adviser (NOTUS).
  • Raphael Chavez-Fernandez ($18K, Gallego): Gallego's chief of staff since 2022 and a senior 2024 campaign strategist (press release). Earlier he was Menendez's New Jersey state director.
  • Of Independence LLC ($15K, Gallego communications): not identified.
